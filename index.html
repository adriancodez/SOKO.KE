WHO WE ARE

Building technology for everyday life.

Soko.ke is a Kenyan technology initiative focused on developing practical digital solutions for everyday needs. Through connected platforms, we aim to make essential services more accessible, convenient and efficient.

01

Our Vision

To contribute to a connected and innovative Kenya where technology creates opportunities for everyone.

02

Our Mission

To develop accessible technology solutions that solve real problems and improve how people live, work and connect.

03

Our Philosophy

People, Reimagination and Think Global, Live Local guide how we approach innovation and build our platforms.
