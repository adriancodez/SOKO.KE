ABOUT SOKO HUB

Connecting people through digital commerce.

Soko Hub is part of the Soko.ke ecosystem, focused on creating better connections between businesses and the people they serve.

The platform aims to make it easier for people to discover businesses, explore available products and connect with sellers through a more accessible digital experience.

WHAT WE AIM TO ACHIEVE

Making digital commerce more accessible.

Business Visibility

Helping businesses establish a digital presence and become easier for potential customers to find.

Customer Connections

Creating opportunities for buyers and sellers to discover and connect with one another.

Product Discovery

Making it easier for customers to explore products and discover businesses in one place.

Local Commerce

Supporting the growth of local businesses by encouraging wider access to digital markets.

OUR VISION

A more connected marketplace.

We envision a digital environment where businesses can reach more people and customers can discover opportunities with greater ease.
