ABOUT SOKO PAY

Simplifying the way people transact.

Soko Pay is a digital payments initiative under Soko.ke, focused on exploring simpler ways for people and businesses to manage everyday transactions.

The platform aims to bridge the gap between traditional transactions and modern digital payment experiences.

By embracing accessible technology, Soko Pay seeks to contribute to a more convenient and connected financial ecosystem.
