ABOUT SOKO TRANSIT

A smarter approach to everyday mobility.

Soko Transit is a mobility-focused initiative under Soko.ke, centred on improving how people interact with transport services.

The platform aims to explore practical technology-driven solutions that make transportation more accessible, convenient and efficient.

By reimagining everyday transport experiences, Soko Transit seeks to contribute to a more connected transport ecosystem in Kenya.

OUR VISION

Making movement smarter, safer and easier.

We believe that better mobility starts with understanding the everyday challenges people face when moving from one place to another.

Through innovation, collaboration and locally relevant solutions, Soko Transit aims to contribute to the future of transportation in Kenya.
